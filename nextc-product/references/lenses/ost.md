# Lens: Opportunity Solution Tree

Source: Teresa Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits*.

**Fits when** there is a clear outcome to move and several possible solutions to choose between.
**Skip when** the question is a yes/no on one idea with no outcome metric, or about market structure.

## Method

1. Define the outcome as a behaviour change ("increase X among Y, measured by Z"), not a feature.
2. List opportunities as needs in the user's words ("I struggle to ..."). Each opportunity cites
   the evidence that it is real, or is marked as judgment.
3. Pick the one or two opportunities with the most evidence and the best fit to the founder's
   assets in intake.
4. For the top opportunity, generate three to five distinct solutions before choosing one.
5. For the chosen solution, list the assumptions that must hold (desirability, viability,
   feasibility, usability) and the smallest test for the riskiest one.

## Anti-patterns

- An output disguised as an outcome ("ship the AI feature").
- A solution disguised as an opportunity ("need notifications").
- One opportunity leading to exactly one solution.
- Agreement between the tree and the founder's plan read as a signal. It only shows the brief is coherent.

## Lens section of the output

Outcome; opportunity list with evidence; chosen opportunity and why; three to five solutions;
riskiest assumption and its test, with a threshold and a denominator.
