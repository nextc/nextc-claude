# Lens: Morphological Analysis (Zwicky box)

**Fits when** the design space is open and the founder may be stuck on the first configuration
they thought of. **Skip when** the shape of the product is fixed and the question is only whether
to build it.

## Method

1. Pick five to seven independent dimensions that are decisions (user type, distribution,
   pricing, input, where the work happens, who authors content). Not descriptions.
2. List three to six options per dimension, including at least one unfamiliar option.
3. Strike out incompatible combinations and say why.
4. Pick the three most interesting combinations. For each, name the closest real product that
   resembles it, from the prior-art records where possible, and score novelty, feasibility and fit.
5. Check every combination against prior art: a combination that already exists is not novel.

## Anti-patterns

- Two dimensions that are the same decision under different names.
- Only obvious options, which produce only obvious combinations.
- Picking combinations because they sound good rather than by stated criteria.

## Lens section of the output

Dimensions and options (compact table); three combinations with configuration, closest existing
product (claim id), and scores; the one combination worth testing and the test.
