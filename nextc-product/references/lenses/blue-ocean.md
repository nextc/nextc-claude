# Lens: Blue Ocean (strategy canvas and ERRC grid)

Source: W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne.

**Fits when** there are several direct substitutes competing on the same factors (the prior-art
pack has three or more). **Skip when** prior art found nothing comparable; there is no canvas to draw.

## Method

1. List six to ten factors the substitutes compete on, taken from their own pages and reviews.
2. Score the typical substitute low/medium/high on each factor, citing records.
3. Fill the ERRC grid: eliminate, reduce, raise, create.
4. Test the value-innovation claim: eliminate and reduce must cut cost, raise and create must add
   value. If only one half holds, say it is differentiation, not a blue ocean.
5. Name the non-customers the move would reach and what evidence says they exist.

## Anti-patterns

- Calling any differentiation a blue ocean.
- An ERRC grid with only Raise and Create.
- Ignoring the cost side.

## Lens section of the output

Canvas table (factor | substitutes | proposed | evidence); ERRC grid; one paragraph on whether the
move is value innovation or differentiation; the non-customer tier it unlocks.
