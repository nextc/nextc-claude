# Lens: Mom Test / customer discovery

Sources: Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*; Steve Blank, customer development.

**Fits almost every question** where demand is not yet proven. It is the lens that turns the
memo's unknowns into conversations. **Skip only when** the founder already has behavioural data
(usage, payments) that answers the demand question.

## Method

1. Name the segment to talk to, specifically, and where to find five to ten of them. Prefer the
   founder's own assets from intake (existing users, a waitlist, an audience).
2. Name the riskiest belief the conversations must test.
3. Write six to eight questions about past behaviour ("tell me about the last time ..."), never
   about the idea or the future.
4. Name the commitment to ask for at the end (time, reputation or money) and what counts as a pass.
5. Write the kill criterion with a denominator: "fewer than 2 of 8 ...".

## Anti-patterns

- Pitching the idea in the interview.
- Counting compliments or "I would pay" as evidence.
- A kill criterion with no number or no denominator.

## Lens section of the output

Segment and where to find them (cite the founder's assets); the belief being tested; questions;
the commitment ask; pass and kill thresholds with denominators.
