Question: should an "expiry reminder" engine for groceries (barcode lookup, shelf-life estimates, reminders before an item expires) be a standalone paid app for home cooks, or a component licensed to grocery-list and meal-planning apps?

What I know and own: a small forum of home cooks who say they waste food, and a working web prototype of the engine. Nobody has paid yet.
What would change my mind: evidence that shelf-life estimates are too inaccurate to trust, or that grocery-list apps already do this.
