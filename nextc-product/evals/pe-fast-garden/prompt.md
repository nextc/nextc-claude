Use the nextc-product product-explore skill with the arguments `--auto --fast`. I am not available to answer questions during this run; use what is below and record anything missing as an assumption.

Idea: "PlotPal", a mobile, offline-first garden planner. One place for a garden's beds, planting calendar, watering reminders, seed inventory, notes and photos, with PDF export. Discovery is not built in: the app links to seed shops and garden-centre sites and earns affiliate commission. Optional encrypted cloud backup up to 20 MB; no account needed by default.

Intake answers:
- Decision: build it or not.
- Assets: a 10-slide mockup deck. I have shipped several Flutter apps before. No users, no waitlist.
- Monetisation I researched myself (unverified): seed shops pay 5-10% per order with a 30-day cookie; one garden-centre chain pays a flat bounty per new customer.
- Competitors I know: Planter, Gardenize, Seed to Spoon. I think they focus on planting calendars, not on complete record-keeping, and many are not free.
- Kill conditions and timeline: not decided.
